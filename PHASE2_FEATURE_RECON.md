## D. What this means for the spec (Phase 3 inputs)

1. Streaming protocol: relay Anthropic SSE events through our own typed SSE (`message.start`, `block.delta`,
   `citation`, `research.progress`, `message.stop`, `error`) and handle `ping`, mid-stream `error`, `pause_turn`.
2. Artifacts: sandboxed iframe without same-origin, strict CSP, versioning, Preview/Code tabs (the existing
   app in this folder already follows this pattern).
3. Research runs need their own entity with steps, sources, budgets (`max_uses`, time, tokens), and cancel.
4. Citations are mandatory UI for web-sourced answers; store `encrypted_content`/`encrypted_index` untouched for
   multi-turn correctness.
5. Shortcuts: provide configurable bindings; avoid copying OWUI's exact defaults verbatim as a "brand" - choose our own.
